# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Robbie Advanced Cleaning Control to be a welcoming, harassment-free
open-source project for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience level or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code, behavior and evidence, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Protect privacy: never post credentials, private entity names, production
  URLs, personal data or unreviewed diagnostics.
- Respect project boundaries, the MIT License and upstream communities.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
misgendering, doxxing and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews and other
spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove
content and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban participants when
needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's **Report a vulnerability**
action on the Security page and label the report as a conduct concern. If that
action is unavailable, open a public `[Conduct contact request]` without names,
screenshots or incident details and request a private channel. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical. Good-faith reporters will not face
retaliation.

This policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
